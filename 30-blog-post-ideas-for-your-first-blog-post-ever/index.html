30 Blog Post Ideas For Beginners

Just started a blog? Take a look at these 30 blog post ideas for beginners to start writing today.

I know, I know, getting started can be tough, huh? So, if you’ve started your blog using our 5 minutes tutorial and perhaps even took care of your blog’s design, you’re now watching at a blank post editor window wondering what your first post should be about… Let me share 30 blog post ideas that will work amazing for launching your first blog post today.

30 Blog Post Ideas For Beginners

1. To begin with, share your life journey until this moment!

2. Then, explain why you’ve decided to start a blog.

3. Follow up with 5 facts everyone should know about you.

4. Share your blogging goals for the first month.

5. 5 of my favourite bloggers that inspired me to start blogging.

6. Storytime. Share a personal story with your audience.

7. 4 Books That Shaped Me.

8. Share Your Outfit Of The Day.

9. 7 Countries You’d Love To Visit.

10. Find Me Elsewhere. Share your favourite places to hang out on social media.

11. A Letter To My Younger Self.

12. Write a Letter to your Future Self

13. Share your approach towards the topic that is really important to you.

14. 5 Ways To Completely Wind Off.

15. Common questions. Answer a few of the questions people that don’t know you usually ask.

16. 5 Topics You Will Find On This Blog. Explain what are you going to blog about.

17. Introduce your people. Talk about your family, friends or relationship status.

18. What I Love Doing On A Friday Night.

19. 3 Things I Will Never Regret Doing.

20. Current Obsessions (Share your Beauty/Fashion/Tv Show favourites).

21. A Day In The Life. Take a picture every couple of hours during your day. Then, share them in a blog post & explain what you’ve been up to.

22. Challange yourself to omplete any type of 30-day challenge and introduce the start of it in a blog post. Don’t forget to keep your audience updates along the way!

23. 10 Life Lessons I’ve learned in School/University/My First Job (pick what fits you best!).

24. What’s In My Bag?

25. Share your hobbies.

26. Bucket List: 30 Things to Do Before You Turn 30 (change a number to any age!).

27. 3 Topics You Will Never Find On This Blog.

28. 5 People That I Look Up To.

29. Gratitude Journal. Share a few things that made you smile today.

30. Finally, create & share your to-do list. Build an action plan for growing this new blog of yours and share it with the world. You can do it!!

Let me know if this helped you to write your very first blog post and make sure to leave your link in the comments!
