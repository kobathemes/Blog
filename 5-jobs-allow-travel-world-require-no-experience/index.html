5 Jobs That Allow You To Travel The World And Require No Experience

Working from anywhere is a dream for many of us! Are you interested in finding those perfect jobs that allow you to travel the world and require no experience? Excellent! This post is for you!

What Are The Jobs That Allow You To Travel The World

Boy, I’m glad I’m able to live in 2018! Old stereotypes of jobs have now changed for good and now you can choose from many jobs that allow you to travel the world even if you don’t have any particular experience in the field.

With many online opportunities and internet connection even in the most remote parts of the world, the sky really has no limits. By creating a content online, selling goods remotely or learning to monetize your blog, you can really make a comfortable living from anywhere!

None of the jobs mentioned in this post requires you to go to university or practice for years in order to start earning money. You can easily get into any of these career paths right this minute and start planning your next travel adventure alongside!

Do you feel excited? You should be! Here are some of the jobs you can start today in order to start travelling the world tomorrow.

#1 Blogger

Building career online can open so many doors for you! Not only starting a blog can be the best career ever, it can also be a perfect way to earn some extra income!

I feel grateful every single day that I get to do this for a living. Here are just some of my favourite things I was able to do because of my blog and I really hope it will give you some inspiration to go for this:

• In the first year as a blogger, I was able to support myself fully while completing my bachelor degree. Even when I was crazy working on my dissertation, my online business brought me enough income to pay my bills and even to celebrate my graduation with some fancy bubbles!

• My fiance and I got to travel to New Zealand and spend almost two months working from the back of a campervan & making a profit every step of our journey.

• I got to spend some memorable blogging time in New York City pretending to be Carrie Bradshaw and writing my top posts in Manhattan’s coffee shops.

• Last year, I even got a chance to buy a property funded 100% from my blogging business.

• What I really appreciate the most is that get to live anywhere I want because of my blog.

• Finally, I get to seriously DREAM. Seriously, this is the best part- the sky has no limits when you’re a blogger!

I think that gives you an idea!

Ok, I know, I know…starting a blog might look like a hustle to you at this point, but in reality- it only takes 5 minutes if you follow this tutorial… Crazy right?

#2 Amazon Seller

Selling on Amazon can actually be a really good way to make some extra bucks.Wondering how this would work? Well, you can simply open an online store, that is run by Amazon. You can be in charge of finding your own products to sell. Once you have inventory to sell. That is sent to an Amazon fulfilment warehouse, where they will store it for you until it sells. Once the product has sold on the Amazon website, the warehouse employees pack that item into a shipping box and send it off to the customer. If the customer needs help with their order, they ask Amazon. Easy-peasy, huh? And the best thing is- you can run your shop from ANYWHERE.

Ok, ok, there’s quite a research you need to do before, but luckily, there are quite a few bloggers that already made awesome businesses from this & you can learn from them!

#3 Virtual Assistant

Are you tech savvy? Do you write kick-ass e-mails or consider yourself to be a pro in googling? You should consider starting your own virtual assistant business. VAs usually take care of the tasks that online entrepreneurs cannot find the time for. For example, you can be doing research for them, booking tickets or answering simple e-mails. Doesn’t sound very difficult, huh? From where I stand, everyone can really do it. All you need is the ability to commit and a few hours to spare in your day.
