5 Virtual Services You Can Offer To Make Money Online

To be fair, if you want to make money online, you should know it is not a get rich quick scheme. Not at all. You will have to put a lot of work in, but if you determine a high-quality virtual service that you can offer to your audience, you will be able to start monetising your online presence right this minute. 

Why I Should Offer Virtual Services To Make Money Online

First of all, making money online can be done in so many amazing ways. It’s not really all about ad placement or affiliate marketing anymore. More and more creators choose to introduce their own services in order to monetise their online presence.

Not only offering virtual services will help you make the first paycheck from your blog, it might even grow into a full-time business sooner than you think.

As most of you probably know by now, I personally offer blog design as well as blog consulting services here on my blog. This allowed me to choose blogging full-time even without any huge audience and most importantly- it allows me to have all the freedom in life that I want.

Consequently, I feel grateful every single day that I get to do this for a living. Here are just some of my favourite things I was able to do because of my blog and I really hope it will give you some inspiration to go for this:

  • In the first year as a blogger, I was able to support myself fully while completing my bachelor degree. Even when I was crazy working on my dissertation, my online business brought me enough income to pay my bills and even to celebrate my graduation with some fancy bubbles!
  • My fiance and I travelled to New Zealand for almost two months while working from the back of a campervan & making a profit!
  • We’ve also got to spend some memorable blogging time in New York City. This is where I’ve got to pretend to be Carrie Bradshaw and create some of my top posts in Manhattan’s coffee shops.
  • In general, we get to travel the world whenever we want, while still managing to make money online every step of the way.
  • We even bought a property funded 100% from this blogging business.
  • Finally, I get to really DREAM. Seriously, this is the best part- the sky has no limits when you’re a blogger!

Well, I think that gives you an idea!

Would you like to start offering your own services? Here are some that can easily help you make money online:

Blogging Services

Let’s start with an obvious one! Offering blogging services to companies or simply monetising your blog content can be a perfect way to make some extra income.

Ok, I know, I know…starting a blog might look like a hustle to you at this point, but in reality- it only takes 5 minutes if you follow this tutorial… Crazy right?

By now you might be wondering what actual services you can offer as a blogger and here are some you might find very interesting:

  • Sponsored Content
  • Personal Shopping
  • Social Media Management
  • Paid Social Media Campaigns
  • Sponsored Reviews
  • Photography Services
  • Anything that comes to your mind, really!

Seriously, these days you can include almost any type of service on your blog, just be brave enough to put yourself out there! If you’re interested to know how exactly I make money online through my blog, read this post.

Virtual Assistant

Are you tech savvy? Do you write kick-ass e-mails or consider yourself to be a pro in googling? You should consider starting your own virtual assistant business to make money online. VAs usually take care of the tasks that online entrepreneurs cannot find the time for. This can include anything from doing research for them to booking tickets or answering simple e-mails. Basically, everyone can do it if you are ready to commit and have a few free hours in your day.

Not sure what exact services you can suggest? Gina put together a FREE list of 150+ VA services for you to pick from over here.

Photo Editor

Do you have great photo editing skills? Have you ever considered editing other people’s pictures? We all can take pictures these days, but not many of us actually have time & skills to make them look professional. Here’s where you come in!

Looking to learn a thing or two about photo editing? Sign up for Adobe Photography Plan & download Lightroom + Photoshop. These might look a bit difficult to use at first, but it’s so worth to take your time & actually learn how to use these. It’s so simple these days, you can find a bunch of handy tutorials on youtube.

Once you develop your skills, go ahead and start introducing your services online. You see, photo editing takes loads of time. Other online entrepreneurs would really prefer to hire someone else to do the dirty work for them. Can this be you? It can become an awesome way for you to make money online!

Amazon Seller

Selling on Amazon can actually be a really good way to make some extra bucks.Wondering how this would work? Well, you can simply open an online store, that is run by Amazon. You can be in charge of finding your own products to sell. Once you have inventory to sell. That is sent to an Amazon fulfilment warehouse, where they will store it for you until it sells. Once the product has sold on the Amazon website, the warehouse employees pack that item into a shipping box and send it off to the customer. If the customer needs help with their order, they ask Amazon. Easy-peasy, huh? And the best thing is- you can run your shop from ANYWHERE.

To begin with, you should know that there’s quite a research you need to do before, but luckily, there are quite a few bloggers that already made awesome businesses from this & you can learn from them!

Check out this FREE 7-day course that will teach you everything you need to know about selling on Amazon. I already joined!

Consultant

Whoever you are- a financial advisor, a dog walker or simply have free time to provide people with personal wake up calls- do it! If you’re already good at something- why not to share it with others? Put yourself out there & it will pay off, I promise!
