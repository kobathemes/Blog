5 Types Of Digital Products You Can Sell On Your Blog

Did you know that majority of my income made online come from my digital products? For those who are not aware, I offer premade Blogger & WordPress themes, branding + media kits as well as custom blog design packages. I believe that selling your own digital products can be a great and very efficient way to monetise your blog. However, coming up with a digital product idea can be a hard task if you’ve never done this before. Let’s brainstorm on this then! In this post, I am sharing five types of digital products that you can create and sell on your own blog.

E-books

Publishing an e-book is way easier than a regular book & it doesn’t require many expenses, which means you can offer a very affordable product and it can be downloaded in seconds! If you feel like you have an expertise in one field or another- consider creating your own digital product by putting an e-book together. Most of the times, people choose to get e-books instead of spending hours doing a research on the topic that interests them, thus the conversion rates are usually pretty great!

Online Consultations

Once you start a blog & begin sharing your knowledge, your readers might have some questions to ask or will start requesting more information on the topics. However, if you start to feel like you keep getting a lot of the same questions and you can see that your knowledge and opinion is valued, you might want to start offering paid online consultations.

From accounting services to stylist consultations- every profession these days has the ability to introduce their services online. If you have a skill you’re good at- go ahead and share it with the world!

Still not sure what exact services you can suggest? Gina put together a FREE list of 150+ VA services for you to pick from over here.

Printables

If you’re a graphic designer, you’re in luck! You can create worksheets, planners, social media packs, textures or prints of your own artwork and allow your readers to buy them for a small fee. Consider something that you will use for yourself, something that will make your life as a creative a bit easier and I bet your audience is going to love it just as much!

E-courses

If you’re ready to go big in offering digital products on your blog- think about creating an actual e-course. From fitness to learning how to make money online, you name it, people are looking to learn new skills and actually pay for the information! Make sure to provide valuable content and listen to what your audience is looking for. This can be a life-changing product!

Lightroom presets

You probably have noticed some of your favourite Instagrammers are now offering their own presets that you can download, upload to Lightroom and make your photo editing way easier. If you get compliments on your editing skills, consider creating a presets pack to offer on your own blog or social media. People are looking for quick ways to update their photographs and this can be super useful for them!

Hope this gives you a better idea of what kind of products you can offer on your blog! If you feel like creating digital products might be for you- go ahead and start putting your ideas into actions! Or if you are already offering any type of products on your blog- make sure to share your link in the comments!
