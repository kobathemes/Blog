Starting a Lifestyle Blog: 5 Rules for Success

Are you planning to start your own lifestyle blog, but feel a bit overwhelmed with all the info you find online? In reality, there are only a few main rules you need to stick to in order to turn your lifestyle blog into a success & here’s 5 of them…

Go With WordPress.org

If you’re taking your blog seriously and want to actually start making money from it- self-hosting WordPress.org is definitely the way to go.

Look, no free wordpress.com blog or a blog built on Blogspot can compare to having your own self-hosted blog. With your own self-hosted site you get all the freedom you need to build a successful online business. You can choose from many available products to make payment process faster or implement affiliate links in few easy clicks. It’s also way easier to trach your growth and gets sponsorships when you run a self-hosted website. This list could go on!

I know, I know, this might sound a bit technical, but with the right resources, you can even launch your self-hosted WordPress site in minutes! Read more about the benefits of migrating to WordPress over here or if you trust my word- go ahead and start a profitable, self-hosted WordPress blog in 5 minutes here.

Have A Niche

There’s a huge problem majority of lifestyle bloggers make when they start out. They tend to blog about everything without setting a definite niche for themselves. Of course, one might say that that’s exactly what lifestyle blogging is all about, but I don’t think that’s really the case. Even if you want to blog about your life in general, I know for sure that there are some particular areas that you feel more knowledgeable in. Setting the right niche for your blog can do wonders for your overall online success.

I used to blog about EVERY SINGLE THING and that kept me back from properly monetising my blog. I have since realised that my passion is in teaching others and that was a game changer. By determining my niche I now know what content works for my audience and how I can help them in the best possible way.

The next time someone asks you ‘so..what’s your blog about?’ you need to give them a clear answer. Read more: How To Find The Right Niche For Your Blog?

Make Sure Your Blog Design Is On Point

Alright, so you have all of your pictures taken, all of your articles written, but the things are still not going in the right direction? Let’s start from the beginning. Type your blog’s domain into your browser & load it on the new tab. You can have the best posts published out there, but if your blog’s layout looks like a mess- I’d leave straight away. Sorry, but that’s the truth. Your potential clients & sponsors also wouldn’t be too impressed. Don’t let this happen to you. Your blog design matters, trust me on this! If you want to become a successful lifestyle blogger, you have to make sure your branding is on point.
