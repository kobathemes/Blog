15 Functional Work-From-Home Business Ideas For Enthusiastic Fashion Lovers

The world is facing a second lockdown these days and I wanted to be helpful and share some of the ideas that can hopefully help you take your work from physical to the online world.

The one thing that Covid has really proved to us is how important it is to have your business online.

Many of the businesses that were relying on physical interaction are now facing difficult times while those that were focussing on e-commerce and online presence are blooming.

What happens if you were a service based freelancer such as personal stylist tho? You don’t have a product to sell per say, so launching an e-shop might not make any sense to you.

You can still make things happen online tho! If you’re a fashionista that want to expand your business to the online world, look through the list below and mark which ideas feel relatable to you.

15 Work-From-Home Business Ideas For Fashion Lovers:

  1. Start a blog and share your fashion tips. Here’s how I personally make money from my blog.
  2. Get into affiliate marketing & start advertising brands you love + get a commission for doing so.
  3. Start a blog to get sponsorship deals.
  4. Offer your services as a personal shopper.
  5. Offer your services as a virtual stylist.
  6. Blog about the latest fashion events & use Google ads to monetize your traffic.
  7. Write an e-book on how to choose the right clothes for your shape and sell it on your website.
  8. Build a capsule wardrobe package with affiliate links & sell it online.
  9. Host webinars and update your audience on ht
  10. Become a freelance fashion writer and pitch your articles to online magazines.
  11. Look into direct sales. Direct selling is basically selling products directly to consumers in a non-retail environment. Thus, you can pitch various fashion items to your audience. Just make sure to find brands that offer this type of service and connect to get the best deals.
  12. Create your own clothes and sell them on your website.
  13. Start a podcast and update your audience on all the latest happening in the fashion world.
  14. Start an online boutique.
  15. Make print-on-demand t-shirts.
